Privacy Policy — Standing

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Standing ("the app") helps you keep track of the direct debits and standing orders that leave your bank account. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is written to be read, not to protect us — if anything here is unclear, please email us using the address at the bottom.

The short version

Standing has no user accounts and no server of its own. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The one exception is the optional AI assistant available to Pro subscribers, which sends your question to Google's Gemini service in order to answer it. That exception is described in full below.

What the app stores

When you add a payment, the app saves the details you type in:

  • The name of the company or person being paid
  • The amount
  • How often it is paid (weekly, monthly, annually, and so on)
  • The next payment date and any start or end dates
  • The category you assign and any notes you add
  • Your app settings, including reminder preferences and whether you have Pro

This information is written to a database inside the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. We cannot see it and we have no way to retrieve it.

What the app does not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not connect to your bank and we never see your bank credentials, account numbers, or card details.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not show adverts and we do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not sell or share your information with data brokers. There is nothing to sell.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the app schedules notifications locally on your device. The reminder is generated on the phone itself — no payment information leaves the device in order to produce it, and no push server is involved.

The AI assistant (Pro subscribers only)

Pro subscribers can ask questions about their payments in plain language. To answer, the app sends your question — along with the payment details needed to answer it, such as payee names, amounts, and dates — to Google's Gemini API. Google processes that request and returns an answer, which the app shows you.

This means that when, and only when, you use the assistant, some of your payment information is transmitted to Google. Google's handling of that data is governed by their own terms, available at ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms.

If you would rather no payment information ever leave your device, simply do not use the assistant. Every other feature of the app works entirely offline, and the assistant is off until you open it.

Subscriptions and payment

Standing Pro is sold as a subscription through Google Play. Your payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your card number or billing address. We are told only whether an active subscription exists for your device.

You can view, change, or cancel the subscription at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

How long the information is kept, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove individual payments inside the app at any time. To erase everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage from your device's Settings — because nothing is held on a server, that permanently and completely removes your data. There is no deletion request to send us, because there is nothing on our side to delete.

Security

Your data lives in the app's private storage area, which Android prevents other apps from reading. Requests to the Gemini API are sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection. No system is perfect, but keeping the data on your device rather than on a server removes the largest category of risk entirely.

Children

Standing is a personal finance tool intended for adults managing their own household payments. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Meaningful changes — particularly any change to what leaves your device — will also be noted in the app's release notes.
